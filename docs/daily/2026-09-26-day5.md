# Day 5 — 2026-09-26

Open-loop multi-horizon rollout and prediction metrics for Project B.
America/New_York date for this note. No planner and no HTTP API.

## Shipped

- `src/worldforge/eval/`: `rollout_latent` encodes the initial observation
  once, then steps the Day 3 residual dynamics under a stored action
  sequence and decodes an observation at each horizon. Horizon 1 matches
  `WorldModel.predict`. Leading tensor dimensions are a batch. The rollout
  stays differentiable. It does not step the environment and it does not
  search for actions.
- `open_loop_metrics` scores a corpus. The target at horizon `h` is the
  stored next observation `h` steps after the first state. Episodes must
  be one observation chain. The aggregate is the mean over episodes that
  reach that horizon and over observation features. The report also stores
  MAE and the population residual standard deviation by horizon (a
  calibration stub). Shorter episodes still count toward the horizons they
  reach. The model is set to eval for the score and the previous mode is
  restored.
- Report format `worldforge.predict.v1`: `horizons`, `mse_by_h`,
  `mae_by_h`, `residual_std_by_h`, `n_episodes`, `n_episodes_by_h`,
  `checkpoint`, and `data`. No secrets and no optimizer state.
- CLI: `worldforge rollout` and `worldforge eval-predict`. Both take
  `--data` and `--out`. `--checkpoint` loads `worldforge.checkpoint.v1`
  and ignores architecture flags. Without it, `--seed` builds an untrained
  model. `--horizon` caps the score. Dataset commands still import without
  torch.
- On the golden fixtures the default score is 6 episodes and horizons 1..8.
  The tests use a smaller hidden width or `--horizon` 2–4 so the suite stays
  fast. Nothing is marked `integration`.
