# Day 6 — 2026-09-26

Model-based planning on the latent dynamics, and a closed-loop regret
report. America/New_York date for this note. No HTTP API.

## Shipped

- `src/worldforge/plan/`: CEM searches a short action sequence. Candidates
  are rolled with Day 5 `rollout_latent` and scored by `regulation_l1`,
  the undiscounted sum of Lotka-Volterra regulation rewards on the decoded
  states. That reward is the negative L1 distance from the coexistence
  equilibrium `(1, 1)`, the same number `LotkaVolterraEnv.step` returns.
  The initial state is not scored. Actions are clipped to the environment
  bounds before scoring and before return. The zero dose is one of the
  candidates, so the returned sequence is at least as good as doing
  nothing under the model.
- The population is a diagonal Gaussian. The mean starts at zero. Each
  iteration keeps the top elite fraction and refits the mean and standard
  deviation. The returned sequence is the best candidate seen, including
  each iteration's clipped mean. Sampling uses a private CPU generator.
  Threads are pinned to 1 and MKLDNN is restored afterward, along with
  the global generator state.
- Defaults stay small for CI: horizon 3, 8 samples, 2 iterations, elite
  fraction 0.25. Closed-loop control replans every step and executes only
  the first action (short-horizon MPC with CEM as the inner search).
- `planning_regret` rolls that controller on the real environment for
  `n_steps` (default 4) and compares the return with a zero dose and the
  Day 1 random dose from the same reset seed. `regret` is
  `baseline_best - planner_return`. Negative means the planner beat both
  baselines. Positive means the better baseline won. An untrained model
  may do either.
- Report format `worldforge.plan.v1` for the closed loop, and
  `worldforge.action_sequence.v1` for one planned sequence. No secrets
  and no weights.
- CLI: `worldforge plan` and `worldforge eval-plan`. `--checkpoint` loads
  a Day 3 directory. Without it, `--seed` builds an untrained model and
  also seeds CEM. `--env-seed` resets the environment and seeds the
  random baseline. Dataset commands still import without torch.
- Tests: on a fixed toy model whose decoded state moves with the dose,
  CEM's predicted return beats the zero sequence and the mean of uniform
  random sequences. The report schema checks the regret identity. The
  same seed repeats. The suite uses a few samples and does not train.
